# Experiment record — replace with a descriptive name

Status: **planned / qualified / running / completed / incomplete / invalid**

This is an operator planning/report template. It does not launch a campaign or
replace a validated runner manifest. Fill every required field before qualification;
use `unavailable` with a reason where a provider cannot expose a setting.

## Question and frozen inputs

- Research question and preregistered benefit/safety decisions:
- Development / validation / confirmation split and custodian:
- Suite version and full Git commit:
- Scenario, variant, track (G/S/R), and eligible steps/probes:
- Scenario/protocol/prompt/charter content hashes:
- Starting source repository, commit/tree hash, and access/license basis:
- Fixture, evaluator, mutation-library, and runner versions/hashes:
- Public requirement lifecycle and fixture-reveal schedule:
- Real-repository adaptation and measured authored LOC, if applicable:

## Treatment, resources, and isolation

- Arms and matched-block/replicate identifiers:
- Agent/model version, reasoning, supported sampling settings and seed status:
- AnaxiGraph version, discovered capabilities, binding, and consultation policy:
- Tools and permissions; separate checkouts, data, caches, and sidecar state:
- Environment image, dependency locks, CPU/RAM, concurrency, and scheduling policy:
- Context rollover, public repair, timeout, and infrastructure rerun rules:
- Per-request/probe time, token, and monetary limits; total campaign ceiling:
- Billing currency/basis and sidecar/indexing accounting:
- Arm randomization and timing repetitions:
- Cold/warm cache definitions and isolated resource allocation:

## Qualification evidence

- Shell/edit/build/browser/checkpoint smoke checks for every arm:
- Baseline and cumulative independent behavior checks:
- Safe full-suite, sound selective, unsafe skip-all, and over-abstraction controls:
- Qualified mutations and clean controls; exclusion rules:
- Mixed-version release, persisted data/queue, and rollback checks:
- Evidence store and immutable logs/artifacts/hashes:
- Outstanding blockers (do not treat missing evidence as passing):

## Analysis and results

- Primary comparison: C minus B. Secondary comparisons and ablations:
- Independent analysis unit, pairing, sample-size rationale, intervals, multiplicity:
- All attempted trajectories, completion/failures, and infrastructure exclusions:
- Safety, independent release, maintenance cost, CI/release latency, and total cost:
- Structural diagnostics and measured/modelled distinction:
- Missing measurements (`null`, never zero):
- Raw evidence references and sanitized result paths:
- Findings, uncertainty, limitations, and corrections/superseded reports:

Leave result fields explicitly unmeasured until execution produces evidence. Do not
infer large-repository performance from growth or controlled-stress results alone.
